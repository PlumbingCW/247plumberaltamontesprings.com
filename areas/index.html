Altamonte Springs and the five towns beside it

Inside Altamonte Springs we cover every neighborhood in ZIP codes 32701 and 32714. Each neighboring city below has its own page, written from that city's own water system, housing and weather history rather than from a shared template.

Longwood and Maitland sit closest, along I-4. Casselberry and Winter Springs lie east along State Road 436 and beyond. Lake Mary is north on I-4. If your address is between two towns, call (407) 964-8940 and a dispatcher will confirm coverage.

How dispatch to a neighboring town works

Every call starts at the same 24/7 line, whichever town you are in. The dispatcher asks for your address, so the call goes to the right technician and the right set of local details, such as which utility serves the street.

Not every address inside a city name is on that city's water. Parts of Maitland, for example, are served by Casselberry, Winter Park or Orange County utilities, and parts of Longwood are not on city water either. If you are not sure who your utility is, check your latest bill. A leak between the utility's meter and your house is yours to fix, and a leak on the street side belongs to the utility.

The same plumbing code applies in every town on this list, the code Florida adopts from the International Plumbing Code (IPC), but each city or county issues its own permits.

If your town is not listed

Neighbors such as Winter Park, Fern Park and Forest City sit close to our dispatch point. Call (407) 964-8940 and give the address. If we can reach you the same day, the dispatcher will say so, and if we cannot, you will hear that right away so you can call someone else.
