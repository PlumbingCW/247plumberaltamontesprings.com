Burst pipe right now: the first ten minutes

Find the main shutoff first. It is usually a round gate valve or a lever ball valve where the line enters the house, or a valve in the meter box near the street. Turn a ball valve a quarter turn so the handle sits crosswise to the pipe, and turn a gate valve clockwise until it stops.

Open the lowest faucet to drain the line, then the highest to let air in. If the valve will not move, do not force it with a pipe wrench. Call us, and call Water Distribution at (407) 571-8340 if the problem is at or before the meter.

What makes a pipe burst

Most bursts come from one of four things: a worn fitting, corrosion, too much water pressure, or a hard impact. Home water pressure should sit near 50 to 60 psi. A regulator that fails high can push a weak joint past its limit with no warning.

Cold snaps can freeze water inside exposed lines and split the pipe wall, though the split often only shows once the ice thaws. Attic runs, garage walls, and outdoor hose bibs are the usual places. Shifting soil and tree roots can crush a buried line, which we cover under water line repair.

Repair methods by pipe material

The right repair depends on what the pipe is made of and how much of it is damaged.

Cracked drain and supply lines inside walls

A drain pipe leak only shows when something drains, which can make it hard to catch. A supply line leak runs constantly under pressure and soaks fast. We open the smallest section of drywall possible and use a moisture meter to track the edge of the damage.

If the pipe fails twice in one season, we look for the cause behind the fitting: pressure, corrosion, or water chemistry. Several failures in one home often mean the whole supply system is aging, and a repiping project may cost less than a string of repairs.

Replacement pipe and fittings should match the IPC-derived plumbing chapters Florida enforces. A permit may apply to larger replacements, so ask the City Building and Fire Safety Department.

Documenting damage for insurance

Your insurer will ask for evidence. Collect it before cleanup begins and again when it ends.

  • Date-stamped photos or video of the burst pipe, the spray, and the water level.
  • Close-ups of wet drywall, flooring, cabinets, and ceilings.
  • A list of damaged belongings with approximate value.
  • A copy of the plumbing invoice showing the repair and the date.
  • Notes on when you noticed the leak and when you closed the main.
  • Receipts for fans, dehumidifiers, or hotel stays tied to the loss.

When flood water is not a plumbing problem

The Little Wekiva River, which runs through Altamonte Springs, hit record flood levels during Hurricane Ian on September 29, 2022, and it overflowed again in Hurricane Milton on October 14, 2024. River or rain flooding is not a burst pipe, and it is not a plumbing repair job. If water is entering from outside, call the county or city, avoid electrical panels, and call us afterward for any supply or drain damage.

Flooding can also loosen fittings and push debris into lines. After a flood event, ask us to pressure-test supply lines and check your hose bibs, since a small crack found early is cheaper than a second water loss. Homes in Lake Mary get the same post-flood check from us.

What we do and do not fix

We repair supply and drain pipes, shutoff valves, and connected fittings. We do not dry out walls or rebuild flooring, so for a big loss you will want a restoration company too. If the leak is a small drip at a faucet or valve, our faucet and valve repair service may be the closer match.
