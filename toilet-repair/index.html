Emergency Toilet Repair in Altamonte Springs, FL

A toilet that will not stop running or is rising toward the rim cannot wait until morning. We take calls 24/7, and while you wait you can turn the shutoff valve behind the bowl clockwise and lift the tank lid to stop the water.

We repair the fill valve, flapper, wax seal, or clog, and replace the whole toilet when it makes sense.

Toilet overflowing right now

Take the tank lid off and push the flapper down to stop more water entering the bowl. Then turn the oval handle on the supply line behind the toilet clockwise until it stops. Do not flush again, since the extra water has nowhere to go.

Put towels around the base and mop up standing water quickly, since toilet water carries bacteria. Wear gloves and wash up afterward.

Why the toilet keeps running

Most running toilets come down to two parts in the tank. A worn flapper lets water leak from tank to bowl, so the fill valve refills over and over. A fill valve that sticks open or a float set too high lets water spill into the overflow tube.

A simple test: add a few drops of food coloring to the tank and wait 15 minutes without flushing. If color appears in the bowl, the flapper is bad. A running toilet can waste 200 gallons or more a day, which you will see on the next bill.

Leaks at the base of the toilet

A toilet rests on a wax or foam ring that seals the bowl to the drain flange. When that seal fails, water seeps out with every flush and soaks the subfloor. The stain on the floor around the base is usually a sign it has been leaking for some time.

We lift the bowl, inspect the flange for cracks or a gap, set a new ring, and tighten the closet bolts evenly to prevent cracking the porcelain. If the subfloor is soft, tell us right away so the flange can be secured properly. The same repair is offered across Casselberry and nearby towns.

Replacement toilets sold today are made to current water-use limits, and the install itself follows Florida's plumbing code (the IPC-based Florida Building Code, Plumbing), for flange height and clearances.

Clogs that a plunger will not clear

A flange plunger and a closet auger clear most toilet clogs. We use a closet auger designed not to scratch the porcelain, and we can pull the toilet if something solid, like a toy, is lodged in the trap. Flushing wipes labeled flushable is a frequent cause of repeat clogs.

When more than one drain in the house is slow, the problem is deeper than the toilet. Our drain cleaning service covers main line clogs, and a camera can show what is blocking the line.

Toilet trouble to look for

Check these before you call so we can bring the right parts.

  • Brand and age of the toilet, if known.
  • Whether the tank water level reaches the overflow tube.
  • Water on the floor after flushing or at all times.
  • A flush that sounds weaker than before or leaves waste behind.
  • Loose seat, rocking bowl, or cracked porcelain.
  • Whether the toilet is a one-piece, two-piece, or wall-hung.
  • Other fixtures backing up at the same time.
