Water is coming up the drain right now

When a drain backs up, the next ten minutes matter more than the next ten dollars. Do these four things before anyone arrives.

1

Stop adding water

No flushing, no dishwasher, no laundry. Every gallon you send down has nowhere to go and ends up on the floor.

2

Check the other drains

Run a little water in the farthest bathroom sink. If a tub or toilet gurgles or fills, the blockage is on the main line, not in one fixture.

3

Lift valuables and rugs

Move anything off the floor near the wet drain. Sewage-tainted water soaks into carpet and cabinet bases quickly.

4

Do not pour chemical cleaner

Caustic drain liquid sitting in a stopped pipe burns skin and can splash back. It also makes the next step harder for the person working on the line.

Signs your drain line is closing up

Most clogs warn you for days. Catching them early usually means a smaller job.

  • Water drains slowly in one sink or tub, and a plunger helps only for an hour.
  • A gurgle comes from a nearby drain when you run the washer or flush a toilet.
  • A sour or rotten smell returns every time the sink empties.
  • Water sits in the shower pan past your ankles after five minutes.
  • Two or more fixtures on the same floor drain slowly at once.
  • A floor drain in the garage or laundry room bubbles or weeps.

Kitchen sink clogs and what actually causes them

The kitchen drain is the most common call. Cooking grease cools inside the 1.5 inch trap arm and coats the wall like candle wax, and each wash of the dishes adds a thin layer. Rice, pasta and coffee grounds stick to that coating until the opening is the size of a pencil.

A disposal makes the problem less visible but not smaller. Ground scraps still end up in the same pipe, and they settle in the first low spot past the trap. If the sink has two bowls, check whether both drain slowly. If only one does, the blockage is usually between that bowl and the shared tee.

We start by pulling the trap and checking the first few feet by hand. If the clog is deeper, a small drum machine with a 3/8 inch cable runs through the wall line. Heavy grease is cut with a blade head, then flushed with hot water so it does not just move down the line and settle again.

Cable, hand snake or jetting: which tool for which clog

The tool should match the blockage. A thin cable can poke a hole through a clog and leave the walls coated, which is why slow drains return a month later. For heavier buildup, a high-pressure water jet scours the full circle of the pipe.

Hand auger (1/4 inch)Sink and tub clogs within 6 to 10 feet of the openingCannot reach the wall line or the main

Shower, tub and drain line leaks

Shower and tub drains collect hair wrapped in soap, usually within the first 12 inches below the strainer. A hand tool often clears them in minutes. If the water returns to the same level the next day, the blockage is deeper in the 1.5 inch trap or the branch behind it.

A drain that clears but leaves a stain on the ceiling below is a different problem. Drain pipe can crack or loosen at a coupling, and water then seeps out each time the fixture empties. When we suspect this, a camera pass and a short run test show whether the leak is in the line, and a sewer camera inspection can follow the pipe farther out if the trouble continues past the house wall.

Apartments, shared stacks and the city sewer side

The city counts 97 multifamily communities and 17,110 multifamily units, so many drain calls here involve a shared vertical stack. If two or more units on the same stack have slow drains, tell the property manager before you pay for work inside one unit. The blockage may sit below your floor.

In the city, Altamonte Springs runs the public sewer that your house or building line connects to, with after-hours sewer backup support at (407) 571-8686. Who owns the stretch between your wall and the street is not something we can state for you. Ask the city's Sewer Collection office at (407) 571-8533 before you assume either way.

Trap and vent layout under a sink follows Florida's plumbing code (the IPC-based Florida Building Code, Plumbing). If a clog turns out to be a trap that was never set correctly, the fix we propose follows that code.

Yard cleanouts and the wet season

The wet season here starts around late May and runs roughly 142 days, based on National Weather Service data for the Orlando area. Saturated sandy ground and leaf litter can push debris into the cleanout cap and slow the exit from the house. Lift the cap only if you can see it is dry and the line is not full.

If you live in or near Casselberry, we reach you from our Altamonte Springs base the same way. Tell us the age of the property if you know it, and whether you have seen roots on a cleanout cap, because that shapes the tool we bring.
