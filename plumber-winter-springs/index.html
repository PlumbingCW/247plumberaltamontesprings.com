A city built mostly in the 1970s and 1980s

When the city last tabulated housing age for its planning documents, using 2000 Census data, the average single-family home had been built in 1987. About 64 percent of its 12,296 units went up between 1970 and 1989, and only about 1 percent predated 1960. Esri figures cited by the county put the total at 16,357 units in 2022.

That puts a large share of homes at roughly 37 to 56 years old today. At that age the plumbing that fails is usually the fittings: supply shutoffs that seize, braided lines that split, a second or third water heater, and slow drains. We cannot say what pipe your walls hold from the year alone, so a technician looks before recommending anything.

West of Town Center, east into Tuscawilla

The original-town streets along SR 434

The city's housing element describes the older western section as quarter-acre lots with lower home values. Older drain lines on lots like these are worth a camera look before a remodel, since mature trees sit close to the pipes.

Tuscawilla and the country-club streets

The Tuscawilla development on the east side dates from the 1970s and includes upscale homes. Larger lots mean long irrigation runs and more yard to hide in, so a slow-moving leak can sit unnoticed behind a high water bill.

Manufactured-home communities

The city's housing element lists mobile home communities such as Hacienda Village and Tuskawilla Trails. Supply lines in these homes run under the floor, so check the skirting and look for damp insulation if pressure drops.

Hardness of about 150 mg/L: where the scale lands

The city's Potable Water page says Winter Springs water averages 150 mg/L hardness, or 8 grains per gallon. That is on the hard end of the scale, so minerals leave a crust wherever water is heated or evaporates. The same page says the city aerates water to remove hydrogen sulfide, the rotten-egg gas.

What to do before we reach Winter Springs

1

Stop the source

Close the nearest fixture shutoff, or the main valve at the house if the leak is large. For a heater leak, close the cold inlet on top of the tank.

2

Kill the heat source

Turn the gas valve on the heater to off or flip the 240-volt breaker. Do not relight a heater that has stood in water.

3

Check the neighborhood

If the street is wet or the water has gone brown, call City Hall at (407) 327-1800 and ask about notices before you drink or cook with it.

4

Protect what is dry

Lift rugs and small furniture off wet floors, set a bucket under drips, and photograph the damage for your insurer. Avoid standing water in a flooded yard.

Our emergency number is staffed 24/7, so you can call mid-step and a plumber will be sent while you finish.

Hayes Road, Gee Creek and the flood record

On September 29, 2022, Hurricane Ian forced the rescue of about 80 residents and 20 to 30 pets over roughly seven hours from a neighborhood near Hayes Road, Holiday Lane, Mockingbird Lane and Lido Road. During Irma in 2017, news coverage reported possible sinkholes on Wade Street and in an apartment parking lot.

A city stormwater plan reviewed in December 2025 looked at 46 areas and named 12 as priority flood-risk locations, with Gee Creek, Soldiers Creek and Howell Creek among the waterways. Wet season starts around May 27 in a typical year, so before June, check that your cleanouts are capped and your sump discharge points away from the house.
