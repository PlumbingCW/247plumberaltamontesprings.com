Fixtures we install in kitchens, baths and laundry rooms

The usual list is toilets, bathroom and kitchen faucets, sinks, tubs, shower valves and trim, dishwasher supply and drain hookups, ice maker lines, and washing machine boxes. Each one needs a shutoff you can reach, a trap that holds water and a drain that slopes.

A kitchen sink with a disposal adds an electrical outlet, a dishwasher air gap or high loop, and a continuous waste line. If the disposal is the only thing changing, garbage disposal repair and replacement covers the swap.

Rough-in, top-out and trim: the stages of new plumbing

Rough-in comes first. Drains and vents go into the slab or floor framing, and supply lines are run through the studs before any drywall. The inspector looks at those pipes while they are still open.

Top-out follows, with vent stacks through the roof framing and the lines pressure tested. Trim is the last visit, when toilets, faucets and valves are set on finished walls.

Underground work in sandy soil

Florida's soils in the north and central part of the state are typically very sandy, according to University of Florida extension. Sandy trench walls slump, so an excavation near a new drain needs shoring or a shallow slope. Call 811 at least two full business days before anyone digs, so buried utilities are marked.

Decisions to make before the rough-in

Changes after the walls close cost far more than changes on paper.

  • Exact fixture models, since rough-in dimensions come from the spec sheet and the standard toilet rough-in is 12 inches from the wall.
  • Where the shutoff valves and cleanouts go, and who needs to reach them.
  • Whether you want a shower valve with a thermostatic or pressure-balancing cartridge.
  • Outdoor hose bib and laundry box locations.
  • Water heater location, plus the vent, gas or electric supply it needs.
  • A written scope that names who pays for patching drywall and tile.

Adding a bathroom or moving a fixture

Drains work by gravity, so a new bathroom has to reach the main drain with a slope of about 1/4 inch per foot. That is easy over a crawlspace or open framing and harder on a slab, where the concrete may need to be cut and patched. A toilet needs a 3 inch drain, and every fixture needs a vent so the trap does not siphon dry.

Hot water is the other limit. A new bathroom group on a 40 gallon tank can leave the last shower cold, so look at the heater too, and water heater installation covers sizing and a larger tank or tankless unit.

Permits and inspections: Altamonte Springs and nearby

In Altamonte Springs, the Building and Fire Safety Department at (407) 571-8446 issues the permits. The city lists a plumbing permit type for work whose plans were pre-reviewed under a building permit, and a stand-alone plumbing permit for work that needs no plans. Ask which fits your job before you buy materials.

Florida's plumbing code is the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, 8th Edition (2023), which is based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code. Lake Mary states that electrical, plumbing, mechanical or gas work requires a permit with no exceptions, and invites homeowners to ask first when they are unsure. That is a good habit in any city.

Neighborhoods, condos and what to expect

Altamonte Springs has 137 single-family subdivisions and 97 multifamily communities. Single-family remodels usually mean opening walls inside your own property lines. Condos and apartments often need written approval from an association before the water goes off, so ask early about shutoff windows and allowed work hours.

Bring photos of the existing shutoffs, the water heater and the panel nearest the work. A technician who sees the layout in advance can plan the route for new pipe and bring the right fittings.
