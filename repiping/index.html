Supply pipe replacement

Whole-House Repiping in Altamonte Springs, FL

Repiping means replacing the water supply pipes inside your house, from the main shutoff to every faucet, toilet, shower and appliance. It is a planned job, usually triggered by repeated pinhole leaks, brown water or pressure that keeps dropping.

We help you decide between a full repipe and a targeted repair, then give a clear price before work starts. Our 24/7 line also takes urgent calls when an old pipe fails before the project date.

Signs your supply pipes are near the end

One leak does not mean a repipe. A pattern of leaks does.

  • A second or third pinhole leak in copper within a short span, especially in different rooms.
  • Brown or orange water that runs for more than a few seconds after a faucet opens.
  • Pressure that is fine at the meter but weak at the showers and sinks.
  • Green or white crust on pipe joints, or stains spreading on ceilings below a bathroom.
  • Gray, blue or black plastic pipe with PB2110 stamped on it.
  • Water bills that creep up with no change in how you use water.

Copper, PEX or CPVC: choosing the new pipe

Each material has a place. The right pick depends on your house, your budget and how long you plan to stay.

MaterialStrengthsWatch for
Copper (Type L)Decades of service, handles heat, does not break down in sunlightHigher parts cost, soldered joints take skilled labor, can pit in aggressive water
PEX (A, B or C)Flexible, fewer fittings, quick to run through walls, resists pinholesDegrades in sunlight, so it must stay covered, and some fittings restrict flow
CPVCLower cost, no corrosion, simple solvent jointsBecomes brittle with age and heat, cracks at fittings

Galvanized steel pipe and why it chokes from the inside

Galvanized pipe is steel coated with zinc. Over time the coating wears, rust forms on the inside wall, and scale narrows a 3/4 inch pipe until a shower dribbles. The pipe can look fine outside while its working bore has shrunk to the size of a straw.

Brown water in the morning that clears after a minute is a common sign. A pipe can also snap at a threaded joint when a plumber tries to work on it, since old steel gets brittle. If a repair attempt keeps exposing more rusted pipe, replacing the whole run is the more honest answer.

Polybutylene: the gray pipe with a known history

Polybutylene is a gray, blue or black plastic pipe that was installed in homes through the 1970s and 1980s. Failures led to a national class action, and a 1995 settlement fund of about $950 million was approved. The pipe tends to fail at its crimp fittings, often without warning.

Check the places where pipe is easy to see: under sinks, at the water heater, and at the main shutoff. If you see PB2110 stamped along the plastic, plan a repipe rather than waiting for a failure. Some insurers raise questions about this pipe, so ask your carrier directly.

How a whole-house repipe is done

The crew shuts off the water, maps the new routes, and cuts small access holes in drywall, usually at fixtures, in closets and behind trim. New pipe is run first, tied into each fixture, then tested under pressure before the old pipe is abandoned or removed.

In a house on a concrete slab, new lines are commonly run through the attic and down the walls rather than under the floor. That avoids breaking the slab. A typical house is done in a few days, and you should ask how the crew will handle water access at the end of each day.

Drywall, painting and who does what

Plumbers open the walls and close them with patches. Texture matching and paint are a separate trade. Ask in advance whether the quote includes patching and primer or only the plumbing, so nobody is surprised when the crew leaves.

Altamonte Springs water, condos and rental buildings

The city treats its groundwater with orthophosphate for corrosion control. That helps protect pipe, but it cannot repair a pipe that already has pinholes. If you are unsure what is happening inside a copper line, ask the technician to cut out a short sample and look at the inside wall.

Altamonte Springs has 97 multifamily communities, and about 10,382 of its 21,196 housing units are renter-occupied per the 2017 to 2021 Census data. If you live in a condo or townhome, check with the association or landlord early. They may control the shutoffs, the risers and the work hours.

What repiping does not cover

A repipe replaces the lines inside the building. The pipe from the meter to the house is separate work, and water line repair covers that. Sewer and drain pipes are a different system and are not replaced during a supply repipe.

Our 24/7 line still answers if a pipe bursts before your project date. Shut off the main valve, open the lowest faucet to drain the line, and call (407) 964-8940. If a leak is near electrical equipment, stay clear and call 911.
